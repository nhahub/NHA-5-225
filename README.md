Project name: (Sales Performance Analysis: Bike & Accessories Retail)

Project description:
Retail sales performance depends on product mix, pricing, customer behavior, seasonality, and market
conditions across countries. The project uses a sales dataset made of three related tables: sales
transactions (order number, product, customer, order, shipping, and due dates, quantity, price, and sales
amount), products (category, subcategory, product line, and cost), and customers (country, gender,
marital status, and birth date). It covers about 60,000 order lines from roughly 27,700 orders placed by
about 18,500 customers between December 2022 and January 2026.
The project will model the three tables as a star schema, clean and prepare the data, and investigate sales
trends across time, products, categories, countries, and customer segments. It will measure revenue,
profit, average order value, order frequency, and shipping time, and identify the factors that contribute
most to sales performance and customer value.
Based on historical sales, the project will build a forecasting model for monthly sales and estimate
expected revenue and order volume for the coming months. All results will be presented in an interactive
Power BI dashboard, together with a final report and business recommendations.
