Project name: (RetailIQ: Online Retail Sales & Customer Analytics)

Project description:
Online retail performance can be significantly affected by seasonality, product demand, customer behavior, 
returns, and market conditions across different countries. RetailIQ aims to transform raw e-commerce 
transaction data into actionable insights through data modeling, data cleaning, preprocessing, exploratory 
analysis, statistical investigation, forecasting, and interactive visualization. 
The project will investigate sales trends across products, customers, countries, invoices, order timing, 
returns, and other operational conditions, depending on the available dataset. It will identify important 
factors contributing to sales performance and customer value, and develop forecasting solutions, 
particularly for predicting expected sales on the next operating day. 
Based on the predicted sales, the project will estimate expected revenue and highlight the expected number 
of orders and units that can be fulfilled. The final revenue and order-capacity calculation will depend on the 
fields available in the dataset and clearly stated assumptions.
